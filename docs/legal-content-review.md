# Legal content and policy review

Before publishing any legal article, checklist, policy or service description:

1. Identify jurisdiction: India-wide, Andhra Pradesh-specific or another named state; do not generalize state land/revenue rules.
2. Check current statutes, rules, official records portals and procedural practice from authoritative sources. Do not invent citations.
3. Identify the reviewer and reviewer qualifications internally and record a last-reviewed date. Do not publish a fabricated advocate name, enrollment number or credential.
4. Distinguish general educational material from legal advice specific to an individual's facts. A public article does not establish that a property was checked.
5. For arbitration, explain that suitability depends on the dispute type, agreements and applicable law; do not promise tribunal authority or an outcome.
6. Ensure pricing, deliverables, exclusions, third-party costs, expected timelines and refund/cancellation terms match the approved service.
7. Do not publish client documents, personally identifying facts, case studies or testimonials without appropriate legal basis and informed permission.
8. Review accessibility, clarity, translations and user-impacting disclaimers.

The site should not represent SVU Developers Private Limited as a law firm, licensed advocate practice, arbitration institution or statutory authority unless independently verified and legally appropriate. Do not describe automated checks as proof of ownership or a title guarantee.
